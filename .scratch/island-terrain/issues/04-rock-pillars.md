# 04 — Meteora rock pillars

**What to build:** A cluster of sheer rock towers near the head of the gorge, Meteora-style: 100–300 m tall, 60–250 m
across, with flat or rounded tops that ruins or a monastery could later sit on, spaced so a glider can weave between
them. A fantasy moment of wonder that is still believable geology.

**Blocked by:** 03 — South-east limestone plateau and gorge

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] 8–15 towers of varied height and girth in one cluster, with gaps of 80–300 m between neighbours
- [ ] Tower walls are near vertical and stay clean at the heightfield's resolution (no terracing or aliasing seams in the game)
- [ ] Collision matches what is drawn: flying into a tower is a crash, flying between them is not
- [ ] At least three tops are flat enough for a later building
- [ ] Reviewed in the game weaving through the cluster, with screenshots
