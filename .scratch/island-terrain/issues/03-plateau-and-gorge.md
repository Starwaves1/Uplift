# 03 — South-east limestone plateau and gorge, generated

**What to build:** Turn the bland rolling hills of the south-east (around km 42–56 east, 32–46 south) into canyon
country, generated by the landscape model rather than drawn on:
- a limestone tableland raised late in the model's history, made of flat-lying hard and soft beds (from ticket 17);
- its river keeps pace with the uplift and cuts a deep gorge where it naturally flows, stepped by the beds, down to the
  sea, where the coast turns it into a calanque;
- its tributaries cut side canyons, and the plateau's edges retreat as stepped escarpments meeting the sea in cliffs.

Target character: Gorges du Verdon, the Causses, the Calanques — reached through uplift, rock and climate, never by
drawing the gorge.

**Blocked by:** 17 — Rock structure in the generator

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The plateau top is broad and mostly flat (karst undulation), clearly higher than the country around it
- [ ] A main gorge 300–500 m deep with stepped near-vertical walls forms by river incision, and it can be flown below the rims
- [ ] It reaches the sea; side canyons join it; the east edge meets the sea in cliffs of 250 m or more
- [ ] No drawn lines: gorge, canyons and escarpments come from the model's uplift, rock and rivers
- [ ] Reviewed in spectator mode from the gorge floor, the rims and the sea, with screenshots shown to the user
