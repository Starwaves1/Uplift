# 29 — Rain and storms: droplets, floods, landslides and debris flows

**What to build:** Weather events that shape the land:
- the rain-droplet erosion driven by ticket 28's precipitation field, and by rain events rather than a uniform
  shower;
- storms, as episodic heavy-rain events, that cause flash floods cutting and widening channels;
- landslides where soil saturates on steep slopes;
- debris flows that run down gullies and build fans.

Materials matter: loose soil and ash move easily, bedrock barely (tickets 17 and 31). The result should be the fresh
scars, scree cones, gully fans and cut banks that make real mountains look alive.
