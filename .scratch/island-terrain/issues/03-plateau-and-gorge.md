# 03 — South-east limestone plateau and gorge

**What to build:** Turn the bland rolling hills of the south-east (around km 42–56 east, 32–46 south) into canyon
country: a limestone tableland standing around 600 m, its edges stepped into ledges and walls, meeting the east coast
in tall sea cliffs. A Verdon-style gorge winds across it, 300–500 m deep with near-vertical stepped walls and a narrow
floor, and ends at the sea as a calanque (a narrow sea inlet between white cliffs). Side canyons feed it.
