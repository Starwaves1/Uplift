# Ground material credits

The terrain's ground layers are built from photoscans by [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under
[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain). Thank you, Poly Haven.

Each layer here is a levelled, recalibrated 1024² reduction of the 4K scan; `assets/fetch_polyhaven.py` fetches the
originals and `assets/materials.py` rebuilds these images.
