# 07 — Nordic upland character

**What to build:** Make the Nordic highland read as glaciated granite fell. Today it is a uniform mesh of fine river
gullies, like crumpled paper, everywhere. It should have broad rolling uplands of ice-scoured knolls and hollows, many
small tarns, bare rock slabs, and cirques and hanging valleys under the north range, with the relief concentrated in the
fjords and troughs. Also fix the ring-shaped lowland lake near km 12 east, 23 south, which reads as an artificial atoll.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Designed fjords

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Fine dendritic gullying on the Nordic upland is much reduced; the upland reads as rolling fell from 300–800 m up
- [ ] Dozens of small tarns sit in scoured hollows (these show as lakes in the game)
- [ ] At least three cirques with steep back walls under the north range, above the Vestfjord and Austfjord heads
- [ ] The ring-shaped lowland lake is gone or reshaped into a natural lake with a believable shore
- [ ] The region is clearly distinct from the Alpine ranges and the Mediterranean hills from the air
- [ ] Reviewed in the game over the fell, the tarns and the cirques, with screenshots
