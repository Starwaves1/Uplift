# 32 — Lots of things to fly around and fly through

**What to build:** The island should be full of things a glider can weave around and thread through: dozens across the
island, in every region, grounded with moments of wonder, and often in chains you can string into a route. No rings.

- **Natural:** sea arches, sea stacks and sea caves on the headlands; rock arches and natural bridges in the limestone
  and on the plateau; windows through thin ridges; fields of rock spires; slot canyons and narrow gorges; gaps
  between peaks; waterfalls to fly past and behind; overhanging cliffs.
- **Built, historically grounded and matched to each region's culture:** stone arch bridges and viaducts, aqueducts,
  towers and lighthouses, castle ruins on crags, ruined arches and colonnades, harbour masts. Windmills (22) and the
  streets between tall village buildings (26) count too.

Where the landscape processes can make a feature, it should come from them: wave erosion leaves stacks and arches on
hard headlands (05), karst leaves natural bridges (03), the plateau leaves pillars (04). Where a heightfield can't
represent it (arches, overhangs, caves), use meshes fitted to the terrain, textured with the rock materials, so
collision matches what is drawn.
